PEDAGOGY:
The "continuity bridge" is a fantastic conceptual model. It breaks down the formal definition (left limit = right limit, and limit = f(a)) into physical, testable parts (left rail, right rail, and the f(a) locking pin). Having the student act as an inspector diagnosing *why* a function is discontinuous (jump vs. hole) directly assesses the MAT.12.2.3.1 objective far better than static textbook problems or the overly simplistic "don't lift your pencil" explanation. The completion screen brilliantly summarizes these three specific math conditions.
