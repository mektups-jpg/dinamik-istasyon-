PEDAGOGY:
The module brilliantly translates abstract derivative formulas into a visual, mechanistic mental model. By treating functions as raw materials moving along a production line, and derivative rules as "cartridges" or "shields" that split and modify these paths, students can intuitively grasp concepts like the product rule's two branches or the quotient rule's preserved denominator shield. This prevents mindless formula memorization and builds genuine conceptual understanding.
